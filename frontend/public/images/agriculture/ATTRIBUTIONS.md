# SevaSathi (सेवासाथी) — Agricultural Image Attributions

This document records the provenance, authorship, original source links, licensing, and placement of all authentic agricultural photographs integrated into the SevaSathi platform.

All photographs are authentic, real-world photographs from reputable repositories (Wikimedia Commons, Flickr Commons) with documented Creative Commons open licenses. No AI-generated or fabricated imagery is used. Generic photographs are clearly distinguished from specific verified storage facilities and market data records.
